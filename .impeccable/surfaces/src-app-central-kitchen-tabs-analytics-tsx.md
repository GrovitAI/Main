# Kitchen Analytics

Scope: Central Kitchen monthly reports. Mode: Operate. Owner and kitchen staff trace monthly goods, purchases and cash to recorded entries. The approved HTML Report workspace is the code-led composition reference; no new visual world or concept round is needed.

THESIS: One working report at a time, separating goods supplied, purchases and cash, with filters, entry detail and reconciled export.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit DESIGN.md and the existing kitchen controls: one Le Laban action blue, pale canvas, flat white surfaces, system type, tabular money and restrained separators. The user's 2026-10-08 refinement promotes Analytics into kitchen navigation; preserve the main app navigation.

STORY: Open Analytics directly from the kitchen rail or phone tab, choose a month and report, narrow a branch/item or category/vendor, inspect recorded entries, then export the same filtered report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 840px of available report width, title, month arrows, info and refresh share one compact toolbar. Totals occupy a slim separator strip, with explanations available through the labelled info control. Report tabs use bounded widths on desktop; filters and category management fit the compact report controls. Web report columns begin at 700px, inline filters at 640px, measured after navigation consumes its width. Phones keep a full-width report selector and Export beside the report heading, expandable filters and labelled quantity/value pairs. Six direct rail sections include Analytics; five phone controls are Home, Analytics, Items, History and More, with Branches and Vendors in More. Large amounts retain their exact values without shrinking. Category selection reveals purchase entries inline.

FORM: User-selected Report workspace from the existing code-led HTML preview, October 8. No seed applies to this precisely specified extension. Signature interaction is a category row becoming a filtered purchase-entry view without losing month context; standard push navigation and short state feedback only.

ROW REFINEMENT: Desktop Sales overview separates Branch, Item, Quantity and Value into four equal flexible columns with matching headings. Phones retain the combined branch/item label and paired quantity/value row. Interactive Sales and Purchases rows use the solid Accent Soft token for hover and press, without fading their content. Entry-detail and Cash semantics remain unchanged.
