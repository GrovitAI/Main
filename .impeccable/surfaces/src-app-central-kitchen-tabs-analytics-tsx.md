# Kitchen Analytics

Scope: Central Kitchen monthly and date-range reports. Mode: Operate. Owner and kitchen staff trace goods, purchases and cash for the selected period to recorded entries. The approved HTML Report workspace is the code-led composition reference; no new visual world or concept round is needed.
Date-range extension (2026-10-08): Reports also accept any inclusive start/end dates through today in India time. Phones place the month/range picker beside Filters rather than above the KPIs. Compact flat KPI cards replace the slim strip, explicitly requested by the user; desktop retains their small existing-library icons. A bounded popup calendar provides start/end selection, previous/next calendar months, a This month reset, Cancel and Apply. Editing a draft never fetches; Apply updates the KPIs, rows, filter options and CSV together. Invalid/reversed ranges cannot apply, future days cannot be selected, and current-month default remains month to date. This is a local surface extension of the incumbent Kitchen Ledger system.
Compact ledger extension (2026-10-09, task 168): The user selected mobile option A from task 167. The implemented phone contract below supersedes the earlier phone KPI icons, labelled quantity/value pairs and direct heading actions. The desktop Report first composition and shared design system are preserved.

THESIS: One working report at a time, separating goods supplied, purchases and cash, with filters, entry detail and reconciled export.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit DESIGN.md and the existing kitchen controls: one Le Laban action blue, pale canvas, flat white surfaces, Source Sans 3 type (selected in task 170), tabular money and restrained separators. The user's 2026-10-08 refinement promotes Analytics into kitchen navigation; preserve the main app navigation.

STORY: Open Analytics directly from the kitchen rail or phone tab, choose Sales, Purchases or Cash first, then set the month or inclusive date range and disclose branch/item or spending/category/vendor filters. Read the period-wide KPI cards, inspect the filtered report and recorded entries, then export the same selection. Changing report retains the selected dates.
